What changed & why

AreaBehaviour
Canonical OG pathsBase.astro emits /og/posts/<slug>.png and /og/projects/<slug>.png, whereas the old route checked blog / work and crashed in Satori. Both apps now share packages/cms-helpers/src/og.ts keyed on publicCollections.
Satori VNodes & WOFF1Passing HTML strings to workers-og ran HTMLRewriter without entity decoding (rendering literal &amp; on social cards) and fetched Google Fonts on every render. Passing Satori VNodes with embedded @fontsource/inter WOFF1 bytes fixes both.
Cache & draft safetyReplaced max-age=31536000, immutable with revalidateable caching + Astro.cache.set(cacheHint) on published hits, private, no-store on preview/edit sessions, and 404 on missing/unpublished entries.
