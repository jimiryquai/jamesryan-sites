What changed & why

AreaBehaviour
Plugin fragments (Base.astro)packages/ui/src/utils/page-fragments.ts escapes attribute values, blocks on*/src overrides, validates external src URLs, escapes </script to <\/script (preserving valid JS/JSON payloads while failing closed on unclosed <!--), and wires EmDash's canonical "head", "body:start", and "body:end" placements via resolveFragments.
CMS project linkstoSafeExternalHttpUrl restricts project.data.url in both apps to absolute http: / https: URLs, omitting javascript:, data:, or malformed links.
Garden Document.astrosanitizeDocumentHtml rebuilds only allowlisted document tags and validated a[href] attributes (tracking HTML5 attribute quote states and foreign self-closing tags), preserving seeded privacy-policy HTML byte-for-byte while discarding <script>, <svg>, and event handlers.
Garden /contact & /404Removed the template POST handler that logged visitor PII via console.log and showed hello@acme.example; gated /contact (and the /404 CTA + /sitemap.xml) on getPublishedRoute("/contact", Astro.cache).

What to watch
