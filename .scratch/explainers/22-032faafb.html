What changed

Why it mattered more than noise

With the staging server in the always-loaded config and no token in the harness's environment, EmDash answered with an OAuth challenge. On 9 November a Copilot helper followed it, opened a login in your browser, and because you were signed in to staging, got an admin grant (refresh token valid to 7 Feb 2027). That's the "my passkey signs me in as admin" moment.

Revoked on 2026-11-12: the two staging token rows and the "GitHub Copilot CLI" client are deleted, along with Copilot's cached files. Production hasn't been checked for the same yet (auto mode refused the read).
