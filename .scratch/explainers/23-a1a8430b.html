A page exists only when it has published content

Ticket 23-a1a8430b · PR #23 · merged to master 2927792 on 2026-11-12 · on staging, not yet on production

What changed

Before this, each route decided for itself, the sitemap hard-coded /projects, and a missing entry fell back to template text (the "Talk to our team" form, "Add pricing content in the admin", the Company Inc. privacy boilerplate). Now one module answers "does this page exist?" and everything asks it, so the pages and the sitemap can't disagree.

What to watch

To launch a page, publish its entry. Contact, Pricing and Privacy Policy appear on the site and in the sitemap the moment their entries are published, and vanish when unpublished. There's no code switch.

Production still runs the old code until you approve the next promotion. Today production answers 200 at /pricing and /privacy-policy with template text; after promotion they 404.

Posts description: /posts now reads the Posts collection's description (Content Types → Posts). It's empty, so the page still uses your tagline until you fill it.

Staging content lags production: staging still has Pricing and Privacy Policy published and the SEO fields from before ticket 18, so the SEO validator fails there on content, not code.

Page builder: its branch is rebasing onto this. Page templates now render through PublishedPage.astro, the one place to switch from Content to Layout.
